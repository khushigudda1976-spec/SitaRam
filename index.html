Enter your measurements

Measured something? Type it in and SitaRam checks it. Leave a box empty to keep its current value. Press the button again later to add more points to the trend.

Upload a CSV of readings

Have data from a wearable or a lab? Upload a CSV with columns such as hr, spo2, temp, sys, dia, glucose, sleep, exercise, radiation. Each row is one point in time.

Upload your doctor’s prescription

Choose a clear photo of the prescription (or a text file), then tap Read. SitaRam turns it into a medicine schedule with a checklist.

Reading photos works best with printed text and needs an internet connection. Always check the result against the paper prescription. If you are unsure, ask the doctor or pharmacist.

Emergency alert

In a serious case, SitaRam asks “Are you okay?” If you don’t answer in time, it alerts the flight crew and sends a help message to ground services.

Demo with simulated data. No real call, alert, or message is sent. Alert limits are illustrative and not medical advice. A real system would use the crew flight surgeon’s approved limits and each astronaut’s personal baseline.
